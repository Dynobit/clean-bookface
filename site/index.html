Remember when your feed was your friends?

A few photos. An ordinary Tuesday. Someone asking who’s around on Saturday. We liked that bit.

Clean Bookface gives your Facebook download a home you control. Keep your history private, share a memory with a friend, and read their posts in order.

No ads. No engagement ranking. No bot accounts.
Just the useful part of a social network, with an exit door.

Want to share with friends? A circle adds accounts and private sharing. You don't have to delete Facebook.

  1. 1

    Get a place to keep them

    A circle is a site run by you or someone you trust. Ask a friend for an invitation. Nobody hosts one yet? Set up a small circle together. If you already have an invitation, open the personal link your friend sent and follow its sign-up steps. Save your recovery kit privately. You do not need a GitHub account to join.

  2. 2

    Ask Facebook for your download

    In Facebook’s settings, find Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export or download your information. Choose JSON, save every part, and keep an original backup. Menu names can vary.

    Facebook’s download instructions ↗
  3. 3

    Open “My memories” in the app

    Select all the ZIP parts together, keep the tab open and check the import warnings. Your browser encrypts memories before saving them to your home. Nothing is shared until you choose.

  4. 4

    Share one thing with one friend

    Accept each other as friends and compare the identity check over a call. Pick a photo or post, review the copy, and choose who sees it. Importing a friend list never contacts anyone.

Taking a break from Facebook—or asking it to delete your account—is a separate, optional step. Check your saved copies first. Read the leaving guide.

Before you settle in

Can I sign up on this website?

This page explains the project; it does not host member accounts or take uploads. Join a circle through a personal invitation, or follow the hosting guide to run one. There is no official public sign-up service.

Who can see my download?

The local book reads selected ZIPs in this page's memory without uploading them. Closing or reloading the page means reopening your originals. Its portable downloads are readable files, so keep them private.

The encrypted app opens memories in your browser. Your storage home gets encrypted copies, but still sees account names, connections and traffic. The app publisher remains trusted: someone who replaces its code can steal keys. Keep your originals and read the privacy guide.

Will it find all my Facebook friends?

No. Imported names are private records, not verified accounts. Exchange invitations or profile links with people you already know. Two people must accept a friendship before sharing. Compatible circles can connect when both hosts enable it.

Do I have to delete Facebook?

No. Downloading your information and importing it here do not delete anything at Facebook. You can keep it, deactivate it, or request deletion through Facebook’s own settings. Facebook’s deletion instructions explain that separate request.

How do you keep bots out?

Personal invitations, posting limits, reports and people willing to moderate. Bots, automated personas and scraping are against the rules. Accessibility tools are welcome. We do not collect identity documents or pretend software can perfectly prove someone is human.

Can I take my memories somewhere else?

Yes. Download your saved imports, bring them privately to another home, or close your account in My account. Friends and sharing permissions do not automatically move. Someone you shared with may have saved a copy; a screenshot cannot be recalled.
