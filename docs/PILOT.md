# A small, temporary place to try it

This is a proposed invited pilot, not an announcement that sign-ups are open.
The donated host starts only after deployment and recovery checks pass and the
owner approves launch. Publishing the source is a separate approval.

The aim is simple: try bringing your memories home, invite someone you know,
and tell us what was confusing. You need a browser and a personal invitation.
You do not need to buy hosting or enter payment details to join this pilot.

## What the invitation will promise

- **A fixed 90-day trial.** The invitation and the site must show the actual
  dates before anyone joins. A restart does not extend them.
- **25 accounts to start**, including the host account. Each account gets a
  1 GiB archive allowance. This is a small trial, so a full Facebook download
  may not fit. Keep the complete original safely elsewhere.
- **Your archive starts private.** Sharing is a separate choice. Joining does
  not automatically connect you to everyone else.
- **No ads or automated accounts.** Invitations, limits and human moderation
  support that policy; they cannot guarantee every account belongs to a person.
- **Time to leave.** For the final 14 days, new imports and sharing stop. You
  can still sign in, download your account, delete content and use safety
  controls. At the closing deadline, account access ends.

Download your account before the closing date and verify that you can open it.
Keep your original Facebook download and a separate backup throughout the trial.
This host is not your only copy of your memories. A download can be imported
privately into another compatible circle; moving your archive does not silently
move every friendship or preserve a different host's identity.

## Who can see what

The host administrator can read data stored on the running server. This
configuration also uses Cloudflare to provide the public HTTPS connection;
Cloudflare terminates that connection and can access traffic passing through
it. This is not end-to-end encryption. Friends can save things you share with
them. See [privacy](PRIVACY.md) before deciding what to bring.

Deleting your account here does not delete your Facebook account. Leaving
Facebook is optional; the [getting-started guide](GETTING_STARTED.md) explains
how to download your information and find Meta's own deletion controls.

## What happens afterwards

The donated hosting ends on the announced date. The open-source project can
continue, and anyone can choose to run a circle. There is no promise of free
hosting forever or of volunteers taking over your account. A community needs
real people who accept responsibility; publishing a repository does not create
maintainers automatically.

Before invitations go out, the host must publish its contact route and the date
when pilot data and remaining backup copies will be removed. Closing the website
alone does not erase its disk. The operator must account for every backup and
record completion privately. Do not promise a deletion date that a backup
provider or retained offline copy cannot meet.

A future limit of 1,000 accounts is a ceiling to investigate, not a claim that
this Pi can serve that many people. Expansion requires measured storage,
import, export, recovery and moderation capacity. If hosting cannot continue,
close cleanly instead of quietly extending the donation.

Hosts: use the [Pi deployment guide](PI_HOSTING.md). Members: start with
[getting started](GETTING_STARTED.md).
