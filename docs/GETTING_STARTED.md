# Bring your memories. Take your time.

Facebook lets account holders request a download of their own information. You need access to the account, and the download may not contain everything you expect. You can keep a copy without joining Clean Bookface or leaving Facebook.

**Downloading, importing and deleting are three separate choices.** Clean Bookface never asks for your Facebook password, signs in for you, or sends a deletion request to Meta. Importing here changes nothing on Facebook.

## 1. Ask Facebook for your download

Open Facebook yourself and go to **Settings & privacy → Settings**. Look for **Accounts Center**, or **Meta Account** if your settings have been updated. Meta is rolling out that change gradually; menu labels can differ between accounts and devices. [Meta's explanation](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/04/meta-account/)

In **Your information and permissions**, look for **Export your information** or **Download your information**. Select the Facebook profile you want, then choose a download to your device.

- Choose **JSON**. This is the format Clean Bookface can read. You do not need to understand or edit the files. An HTML-only download will not import.
- Choose **All time** if you want your full available history, or a smaller date range if you prefer.
- Select the categories and photo quality you want to keep. Higher quality uses more storage. Selecting all categories does not mean this app supports them all.
- Submit the request and wait for Facebook to prepare it. When it is ready, download **every part** before the download expires.

Use [Facebook's download help](https://www.facebook.com/help/212802592074644) if the menus differ. It may require signing in on Facebook. If you cannot access the account, use Facebook's account recovery; Clean Bookface cannot recover or export it for you.

## 2. Keep an original copy first

Save the complete download privately, with a second backup somewhere you control. These files can contain private conversations, contact details and location information. Never attach them to a GitHub issue or send them to someone offering to fix an import.

Check that the downloaded files open and include the photos and periods that matter to you. An import report cannot prove that Facebook supplied everything. Keep unsupported files too; the original download remains your independent copy.

## 3. Join a circle you trust

Ask a friend who runs Clean Bookface for an invitation. Follow that invitation, choose your account password, and save your recovery codes privately. You do not need a terminal, a hosting account or payment details to join a friend's circle.

Ask who runs that particular server before uploading. **The host administrator can read the stored files and database.** Other members cannot browse your private archive through the app. The service is not end-to-end encrypted. You can also [run your own circle](INSTALL.md); the [hosting guide](HOSTING.md) explains costs and responsibilities. There is no official public hosting service yet.

## 4. Import privately, then check the result

Open **Bring your history**. Upload your ZIP, or select the full export folder. Keep files and folders named as Facebook supplied them.

If Facebook gives you more than one ZIP file:

- Put **all the ZIP files in one folder** on your computer. You do not need to open them.
- Choose the folder option on **Bring your history**, then select that folder. Keeping every part together helps the app find the photos that belong with your posts.
- If you already unzipped the files, select a folder containing all the unpacked parts instead. Keep the ZIP files outside that folder so you do not upload both copies.

Folder upload needs JavaScript enabled in your browser; uploading a single ZIP does not. Check the size shown on the upload page. If your complete download is too large, ask the host for help or request a smaller date range from Facebook. Do not leave out files just to make it fit.

You can leave the page while the import runs. When it finishes, read the report, including skipped records, failures and missing-media warnings. Look in **Your memories** and check a few photos, dates and stories yourself. Keep the originals even after a successful import. Videos and unsupported export categories do not become a complete replacement for Facebook's features. Encrypted Messenger history may require a separate Meta export and is not guaranteed to import here.

Nothing is published by importing. You can stop here and use the private archive.

## 5. Bring one friend and share one thing

Send an invitation or exchange Clean Bookface profile addresses, then accept each other. An imported friend list does not recreate friendships or contact anyone automatically. Friends on different hosts need both hosts to support and enable connections.

Open a post or photo, preview its sharing copy, and choose the audience. Start with one trusted friend. Messages and imported friend records have no sharing action. A friend can save what you share, and their host can read its stored copy. New friends do not automatically see old posts.

## 6. Decide what you want to do with Facebook

Keeping Facebook is fine. So is taking a break. Leaving is optional and is never a condition of using Clean Bookface.

| Your choice | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Keep using Facebook | No change is needed. Your imported copy is separate. |
| Take a break | Read Meta's [temporary deactivation instructions](https://www.facebook.com/help/214376678584711). Deactivation is different from permanent deletion. |
| Request account deletion | First finish and verify your downloads and independent backups. Then read Meta's [account deletion instructions](https://www.facebook.com/help/224562897555674) and make the request yourself on Facebook. |

Before requesting deletion, set up another way to sign in to services that use **Login with Facebook**. Review what you need from Messenger and any Pages, groups, business tools or devices you manage. Save what matters and arrange any necessary administrator handover.

In Accounts Center, the usual deletion path is **Personal details → Account ownership and control → Deactivation or deletion**. Choose the intended **Facebook profile**, not another linked account. If your Meta Account menus differ, use the help article and read the scope on the confirmation screen. Follow Meta's current cancellation window and completion terms rather than assuming deletion is immediate.

Some information can remain in recipients' conversations, backups or required retention. Neither a request nor this app can verify complete erasure from Meta's systems. Deleting your Clean Bookface account does not delete Facebook; deleting Facebook does not delete the independent copy here. Clean Bookface has its own export and deletion controls in **Settings**.

## If you get stuck

- **The export is HTML:** request a JSON download from Facebook.
- **Photos are missing:** check you downloaded every part, selected the complete folder and read the import warnings.
- **The file is too large:** read the displayed upload limit and ask the host; do not upload it to a public file-sharing link for support.
- **You cannot find an old friend:** exchange invitations directly. There is no central matching service.
- **You want to move or leave:** use the account export in Settings and read [portability](PORTABILITY.md). Wait for imports to finish before exporting.

Guidance reviewed on 4 October 2026. Meta's public newsroom confirms download controls and the Meta Account rollout. Its Help Center may require login; these instructions are not a claim that every current account's menus were tested. The linked Meta help and your account's confirmation screens control the actual export or deletion process.
